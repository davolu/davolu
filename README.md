### 👋 About Me

I'm a **full-stack developer and AI/ML engineer** from **Lagos, Nigeria**. I build across the whole stack — from clean React/Next.js frontends to Python and Node backends — and I spend most of my time these days on **agentic AI systems**: LangChain pipelines, multi-agent orchestration, and turning models into products people actually use.

- 🤖 Currently building **agentic architectures** — coordinator/sub-agent patterns, task decomposition, and workflow orchestration
- 🧠 Working with **LangChain, RAG, computer vision (OpenCV), and classical ML**
- 🌐 Comfortable end-to-end: **frontend, backend, databases, and deployment**
- 📫 Reach me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-oluyale)
